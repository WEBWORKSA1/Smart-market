Support FAQ

How do I pay?

Send a pledge with the form and we'll email you a secure payment link (card or PayPal). Monthly memberships can be cancelled anytime.

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. Smart.Market is a for-profit publisher, so contributions are not charitable donations. You'll receive a receipt for your records.

Does supporting affect championship results?

No. Supporting never changes rankings, starting cash or prize eligibility. Every player competes on equal terms.
