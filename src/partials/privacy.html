Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what Smart.Market ("we") collects, why, and your choices. It applies to smart.market, our newsletters and our YouTube channel.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone, postal code, age range, approximate investable assets, goals and messages you submit through our forms (advisor matching, newsletter, Championship, contact, partner and careers forms).
  • Automatic data: device, browser, pages viewed, referring URL and approximate location, collected by cookies and similar technologies.
  • Local storage: your watchlist, alerts, theme and Championship portfolio are stored in your browser and not sent to us unless you create an account.

How we use it

  • To deliver what you asked for: newsletters, advisor introductions, contest entries, replies.
  • To share your details with the up to three matched advisors when you request matching. We never sell your data to lists.
  • To measure and improve the site, prevent fraud and comply with law.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings or aboutads.info. We use Google Analytics to understand traffic. Non-essential cookies load only after you accept them in our consent banner. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see a Google-certified consent management platform.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, India DPDP Act), you may access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to or limit processing. Email privacy@smart.market; we respond within 30 days.

Retention and security

We keep lead and contact data for up to 24 months, then delete or anonymise it. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and access is restricted to staff who need it.

Children

Smart.Market is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact
