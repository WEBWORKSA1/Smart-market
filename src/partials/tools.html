How to use these calculators

Compound interest shows how contributions and returns stack over time. The gap between the grey (what you put in) and blue (growth) areas is the payoff for starting early.

Retirement / FIRE uses a safe-withdrawal rate: divide yearly spending by the rate (4% means 25× spending) to get your target nest egg.

Mortgage and Loan / EMI use standard amortization. Early payments are mostly interest, which is why extra payments in the first years save the most.

Dividend income projects cash flow with optional reinvestment. Position size caps the loss on any trade at a fixed % of your account. Inflation shows how much buying power cash loses each year.
