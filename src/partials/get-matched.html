Why fiduciary matters

A fiduciary is legally required to put your interests first. Commission salespeople are not, and a 1% difference in fees can cost six figures over a career.

How we vet advisors

We check registrations (SEC/FINRA BrokerCheck, provincial regulators), disciplinary history, credentials (CFP®, CFA, CPA) and fee transparency before an advisor can join.

How we get paid

Partner advisors pay Smart.Market a fee for introductions. You pay nothing to us, and our fee doesn't change the price you're quoted.
