About Smart.Market

Free, honest tools and plain-English education to help people make smarter money decisions, and a direct line to vetted professionals when they need one.

Our mission

Most financial websites are built to sell you something first and teach you second. We reverse that. Our core tools (market data, the screener, calculators, the Academy and the Championship) are free with no paywall. We earn money transparently through advertising, disclosed partner referrals and reader support.

Editorial standards

  • Expert review. Guides are written or reviewed by credentialed professionals (CFP®, CFA, CPA) and show the reviewer's name and credentials.
  • Primary sources. We cite regulators, filings and official data, and date every figure.
  • Separation. Advertisers and partners never see or approve editorial content before publication. Sponsored content is always labelled.
  • Corrections. Found an error? Email corrections@smart.market. We fix verified errors within 48 hours and note the change on the page.
  • No hype. We never promise returns, never say "guaranteed", and never publish price predictions as fact.

How we make money

Display advertising (Google AdSense), YouTube, newsletter sponsorships, referral fees from brokers and advisors listed on our site (always disclosed), optional Pro features, and voluntary reader support. Compensation can affect where products appear, but never our ratings.

Not financial advice

Smart.Market is an educational publisher, not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Nothing here is a personal recommendation. Consult a licensed professional about your situation.
