Choosing a broker: FAQ

Are $0-commission brokers really free?

Stock and ETF trades usually are. Brokers earn from payment for order flow, interest on cash, margin lending, options contracts, FX conversion and premium tiers. Check the fee schedule for what you'll actually use.

Is my money protected if a broker fails?

In the US, SIPC covers securities up to $500,000 (including $250,000 cash) per customer. In Canada, CIPF provides similar coverage. It does not protect against market losses.

Which account should I open first?

Usually a tax-advantaged account (Roth IRA / 401(k) in the US; TFSA / RRSP in Canada) before a taxable account. Use our decision-tree guide in the Academy.
