Support FAQ

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. Smart.Market is a for-profit publisher, so contributions are not charitable donations. You'll get a receipt for your records.

Can I cancel a monthly membership?

Yes, anytime, in one click from your receipt email. No questions asked.

Does supporting affect Championship results?

No. Supporting never changes rankings, starting cash or prize eligibility. Every player competes on equal terms.
