Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. A purchase or donation does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.
  • Eligibility: Open to individuals who have reached the age of majority where they live. Employees of Smart.Market and sponsors are not eligible. One account per person; duplicate accounts are disqualified.
  • Play: Each player starts with $100,000 in virtual cash. No real money is invested. Prices are delayed or simulated and may differ from exchange prices.
  • Winners: Ranked by % return at season close. Ties are broken by lower portfolio volatility, then by earlier entry. Winners are verified before prizes are paid.
  • Skill contest: Winners are chosen by skill, not chance. Where local law requires it, winners must answer a mathematical skill-testing question.
  • Prizes: Paid within 30 days by PayPal or bank transfer. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.
  • Regional rules: Some jurisdictions add registration or filing requirements for contests. Check the full rules for your region before entering.
