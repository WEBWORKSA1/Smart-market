Questions, answered

Is Smart.Market really free?

Yes. Market data, screener, calculators, the Academy, the Championship and advisor matching are free. We earn from advertising, partner referrals (disclosed on every page where they appear) and voluntary reader support.

How does advisor matching work, and what does it cost me?

You answer a few questions; we introduce up to three advisors from our vetted network who fit your goals and location. It costs you nothing. Partner firms pay us a fee for introductions, and that fee never changes what you pay them.

Is the Championship real money?

No. Everyone trades a $100,000 virtual portfolio. Prizes are real and funded by sponsors. No purchase is necessary; see the official rules.

Is this financial advice?

No. Smart.Market provides education and tools. We don't know your full situation, so talk to a licensed professional before acting on anything you read here.
