# Smart.Market: Business Blueprint & Phase-Wise Build Prompts

Prepared September 2026. This covers the concept decision, revenue model, research findings from 28 benchmark sites, the risks that matter, and copy-paste prompts to build and expand the site one phase at a time.

---

## 1. The decision: what Smart.Market should be

**Smart.Market = a free "smart money" hub.** It combines live market intelligence, free money tools, a monthly investing championship, and advisor/broker matching as the main revenue engine.

Positioning line: *"Make the smart move with your money."*

### Why this beats the other readings of the domain

Each concept was scored 1–5 on six criteria, weighted as shown.

| Concept | AdSense RPM | Lead value | Traffic ceiling | Moat / repeat visits | Build cost | Fit with "smart" + "market" | **Weighted** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Smart-money market hub + advisor/broker matching** | 5 ($20–50 finance RPM) | 5 ($35–$680 per lead) | 5 | 4 (daily market habit) | 3 | 5 | **4.6** |
| Deal/shopping comparison ("smart market" for buyers) | 2 ($3–8) | 2 (1–5% affiliate) | 5 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 2.9 |
| B2B "smart marketing" SaaS directory | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2.9 |
| Local classifieds marketplace | 1 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 1 (cold-start problem) | 3 | 2.2 |
| Prediction-market / crypto signals | 3 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3.0 (policy risk: AdSense, YMYL) |

Weights: RPM 20%, lead value 25%, traffic 20%, moat 15%, build 10%, fit 10%.

**The deciding factor is lead value, not AdSense.** A US, UK or Canadian finance visitor earns $20–50 per 1,000 pageviews from AdSense. One financial-advisor lead is worth $80–$105 on SmartAsset's current subscription model, and historically up to $680. A funded brokerage account pays $30–$250 CPA. **AdSense covers hosting costs; leads are the business.** The site is designed around that: the "Get Matched" funnel is linked from every page, every calculator, the stock drawer and a sticky bar.

### Unit economics per 1,000 visitors (model assumptions, not guarantees)

| Stream | Assumption | $ per 1,000 visitors |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | 60% tier-1 traffic, blended page RPM $12, 2.2 pages/visit | ~$26 |
| Advisor leads | 0.5% complete the funnel × $60 blended sale price | ~$300 |
| Broker/app affiliate | 0.2% funded accounts × $75 CPA | ~$150 |
| Newsletter | 3% subscribe; later sold at $35–$100 CPM finance sponsorship | builds an asset |
| YouTube | finance RPM $15–26 (US) | separate channel economics |
| Donations/memberships | 0.1% × $15 | ~$15 |
| **Total** | | **~$490 / 1,000 visitors** |

At 100K visitors a month, that is roughly $49K a month. About 90% of it depends on leads and affiliates actually converting and selling.

**The main risk:** a lead is only worth money once a buyer exists. Before scaling traffic, sign 2–3 lead buyers (a lead network, an advisor-marketing platform, or 5–10 advisors directly). Until then, send leads to an affiliate advisor-matching program so none go unsold.

### Go-live checklist: `src/assets/config.js`

1. `adsenseClient` and `adSlots`, then put your publisher ID in `dist/ads.txt`.
2. `endpoints.lead` and the others: Formspree, a Make/Zapier webhook, or Google Apps Script → Sheet/CRM. Or set `netlifyForms: true` on Netlify.
3. `donate.*`: Stripe Payment Links (one per amount and tier) plus BMC / Ko-fi / PayPal.
4. `videos[].id` and `youtubeChannel`.
5. `affiliates.broker1..6`: replace "Broker One…Six" in `data.js` with your approved affiliate partners.
6. `gaId`.
7. Run `python3 build.py` and deploy `dist/`.

## 4. Risks to handle before scaling (direct)

1. **Paying for advisor referrals is regulated.** In the US, an adviser who pays for referrals triggers SEC Marketing Rule disclosure duties, and state solicitor rules can apply. SmartAsset runs this through an SEC-registered entity. Start by selling through established lead networks or advisor-marketing platforms that carry the compliance burden. Take securities-counsel advice before selling leads directly to RIAs.
2. **Telemarketing consent.** Keep explicit written consent to be called or texted, naming the parties, on the phone step (it's already in the funnel). Log the consent text, timestamp, IP and page URL with every lead. It is your defence against TCPA claims.
3. **Contests.**
   - Keep the Championship skill-based and free ("no purchase necessary"). A donation must never improve a player's odds; the current copy already guarantees this.
   - Canadian winners need a skill-testing question.
   - Contests open to **Quebec residents** have registration and duty obligations with the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux. Either comply or exclude Quebec in the rules.
4. **Market data licensing.** The site runs on a demo engine. Showing real exchange prices requires a licensed feed, and redistribution terms vary. Use a provider that permits public display of delayed data, or TradingView's free embed widgets (already wired in: `tradingViewWidgets: true`).
5. **AdSense rules for finance sites** (finance is a "Your Money or Your Life" topic, held to stricter review).
   - Launch with 20–30 original, sourced, dated articles and named authors. Don't apply with an empty Academy.
   - Never auto-generate thin ticker pages. Keep any generated pages `noindex` until they have original analysis.
   - No "guaranteed returns" language anywhere.
6. **Traffic geography.** An India visitor earns $0.50–$5 page RPM against $15–$40 for a US one. Target US and Canada keywords (401(k), RRSP, TFSA, IRA) for the money pages. Put global and India content on the Academy and Markets pages, which still build scale.

### Phase 3: The lead engine that makes money (Weeks 3–8)

```
Turn the Get Matched funnel into a revenue system.

1. BACKEND: Supabase (Postgres) tables: leads (all funnel fields, consent_text,
   consent_ts, ip, user_agent, page_url, utm_*, score, status, buyer_id, price),
   buyers (name, geos[], min_assets, goals[], price_per_lead, daily_cap, webhook_url,
   email), deliveries (lead_id, buyer_id, delivered_at, response, refunded).
2. VERIFICATION: SMS OTP on the phone step (Twilio Verify). Email syntax + MX check.
   Reject disposable domains. Deduplicate by phone/email within 90 days.
3. SCORING: 0-100 lead score = assets band (40) + timeline (20) + goal fit (15) +
   verified phone (15) + engagement (10: calculators used, pages viewed). Store it.
4. ROUTING: ping-post to buyers matching geo/assets/goal, highest bid first, cap 3 buyers
   per lead (shared) or 1 (exclusive at 2.5x price). Fallback: send to affiliate
   advisor-matching partner link on the results page.
5. BUYER PORTAL: simple password-protected page for buyers: leads received, dispute a lead
   within 7 days (auto-credit rules), invoices, cap controls. Stripe invoicing weekly.
6. NURTURE: leads that don't book get a 5-email sequence (day 0/1/3/7/14) + SMS reminder
   (with STOP handling). Non-qualified (<$50K) get the Playbook + broker comparison instead.
7. ANALYTICS: GA4 events for each funnel step; a dashboard of step drop-off, cost per lead
   by channel, revenue per lead, and buyer acceptance rate.
Log every consent record immutably. Add a compliance footer on the results page naming
the compensation relationship.
```
