How to read this page

Indices such as the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and S&P/TSX Composite summarize how a whole market performed. Futures trade nearly around the clock and show where markets may open. Bond yields move opposite to bond prices; a rising 10-year Treasury yield usually raises mortgage rates and pressures expensive growth stocks. The VIX measures expected volatility in the S&P 500 over the next 30 days: readings under 15 are calm, above 30 signal stress.
