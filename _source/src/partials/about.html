About Smart.Market

Free, honest tools and plain-English education to help people make smarter money decisions, with a direct line to licensed professionals when they need one.

Our mission

Many financial websites are built to sell first and teach second. We try to do the opposite. Our core tools (live market data, the daily brief, the screener, calculators, the Academy and the championship) are free with no paywall. We earn money openly through advertising, disclosed partner relationships and voluntary reader support.

What's automated, and what isn't

Market data on Smart.Market updates automatically. Live charts, heatmaps and calendars refresh in your browser, and an automated job rebuilds our market tables, screener and daily brief after every trading session. The daily brief is generated from the data with fixed rules: it reports what happened and never predicts or recommends.

Our Academy guides are different: they're researched, written and reviewed by people, with worked examples checked by calculation, and each shows the date it was last updated.

Editorial standards

  • Primary sources. We cite regulators, official agencies and filings (for example the IRS, CRA, SEC, FINRA and central banks) and date every guide.
  • Checked numbers. Every worked example is calculated and re-checked before publication.
  • Separation. Advertisers and partners never see or approve editorial content before publication. Sponsored content is always labelled.
  • No hype. We never promise returns, never call an investment "guaranteed" and never present price predictions as fact.
  • Corrections. Found an error? Tell us. We fix verified errors promptly and note significant changes on the page.

How we make money

Display advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, referral fees from professionals or providers we introduce or list (always disclosed), and voluntary reader support. Compensation may influence where an offer appears, but never the substance of our guides.

Not financial advice

Smart.Market is an educational publisher, not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer or financial planner. Nothing on this site is a personal recommendation. Consult a licensed professional about your situation.
