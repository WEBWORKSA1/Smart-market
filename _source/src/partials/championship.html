Official rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. A purchase, pledge or donation does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.
  • Eligibility: individuals who have reached the age of majority where they live. Employees of Smart.Market and prize sponsors are not eligible. One registration per person; duplicate entries are disqualified.
  • Play: each player starts the season with $100,000 in virtual cash. No real money is invested. Stock and commodity prices are based on the latest daily close and crypto prices on live public quotes; prices may differ from exchange prices.
  • Season: each calendar month is a season. Standings lock at 4 p.m. ET on the last day of the month.
  • Winners: ranked by percentage return. Finalists submit their portfolio for verification. Ties are broken by lower portfolio volatility, then earlier registration.
  • Skill contest: winners are chosen by skill, not chance. Where required by law (for example in Canada), winners must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question.
  • Prizes: funded by sponsors and announced before each season closes. Paid within 30 days of verification. Winners are responsible for applicable taxes.
  • Regional rules: some jurisdictions add registration or filing requirements for contests (for example, contests open to residents of Quebec). Where those requirements haven't been met, residents of that jurisdiction may play but are not eligible for prizes.
