Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 08, 2026

This policy explains what information Smart.Market ("we", "us") collects, why, how it's shared and the choices you have. It applies to smart.market and its email communications.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through our forms: name, email, phone number, postal code, age range, investable-asset range, goals and messages (advisor consultation requests, email list, championship registration, pledges, partnership, careers and contact forms).
  • Automatically collected information: device and browser type, pages viewed, referring page, approximate location and similar data collected through cookies and similar technologies, by us and by our advertising and analytics partners.
  • Information stored only in your browser: your watchlist, theme, calculator inputs and championship portfolio are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent to us.

How we use information

  • To respond to you and deliver what you asked for (consultation introductions, email updates, contest administration, pledges).
  • When you request a consultation, to share your request with up to three licensed professionals we believe fit your needs. We never sell your personal information to data brokers or mailing lists.
  • To operate, secure and improve the site, measure usage and prevent fraud and spam.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the site free.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info (US), youradchoices.ca (Canada) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU). Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a Google-certified consent message and can accept, reject or manage advertising cookies. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work without them.

Embedded content and third-party services

Some pages embed content from third parties, including TradingView (market widgets), YouTube (videos, loaded in privacy-enhanced mode only when you play them), CoinGecko (crypto prices), Frankfurter (currency rates) and Google Fonts. These services may collect data under their own privacy policies when their content loads. Form submissions are delivered to us through a secure form-processing service.

Sharing

We share personal information only: with professionals you ask us to introduce you to; with service providers who help us run the site (hosting, form delivery, email), under confidentiality obligations; when required by law; or in connection with a sale or transfer of the website, in which case the buyer would be bound by this policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, California's CCPA/CPRA, Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25 or India's DPDP Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information and to object to or limit certain processing. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". We respond within 30 days.

Retention and security

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to handle your request and for up to 24 months afterwards, then delete or anonymize them. Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections and access is limited to people who need it.

Children

Smart.Market is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use our contact form (topic: "Privacy request").
