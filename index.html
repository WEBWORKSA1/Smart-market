Questions, answered

Is Smart.Market free?

Yes. Market data, the daily brief, screener, calculators, the Academy, the championship and advisor requests are free. We earn from advertising, disclosed partner relationships and voluntary reader support.

Where does the market data come from, and how fresh is it?

Live charts, heatmaps and calendars are provided by TradingView. Crypto prices come from CoinGecko and currency rates from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter. Our own tables, screener and daily brief are rebuilt automatically from public market data every trading day. Some data is delayed; each section says where it comes from.

How do advisor requests work?

You answer a few questions and send a request. We review it and introduce you to licensed professionals who fit your needs. It costs you nothing, and you choose whether to meet anyone. We may be compensated by partner firms; that never changes what you pay.

Is this financial advice?

No. Smart.Market provides education and tools. We don't know your full situation, so speak with a licensed professional before acting on anything you read here.
